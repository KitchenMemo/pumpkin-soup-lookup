Quick reference

  • Reference recipe: 500 g peeled pumpkin + 500 g water, 4 portions, 30 min total (official Cookidoo pumpkin soup).
  • Thickness control: weigh the edible pumpkin, hold some liquid back, thin after blending.
  • Before blending: largest pieces must pierce easily with a fork.
  • Season last: stocks vary widely in salt.
  • Roast-first starting point: 200°C (400°F), judge by fork-tender + browned edges, not the clock.
  • Soup cook temp: max 100°C (212°F) per Vorwerk's TM6 announcement.
  • TM6 at 95°C+: simmering basket, not the tight-fitting measuring cup.
  • Hot blending: raise speed gradually; never exceed max fill line.
  • Fridge: 3–4 days · Freezer: ~2–3 months (quality guide).
  • Reheat to 74°C (165°F). Out of the fridge max 2 h (1 h above 32°C / 90°F).

Troubleshooting

Change one thing at a time and taste in between. These are suggested corrections, not verified results.
Too wateryToo much stock, moisture released by the pumpkin, or not enough vegetable bodyAdd cooked potato or another suitable thickener, or reduce using a method your model's instructions permitHold back some liquid until after blending
Too thickToo little liquid, or soup stiffened after chillingAdd reserved liquid gradually until you reach the texture you wantWeigh pumpkin and liquid; add in stages
BlandUnderseasoning, mild stock, no contrastAdjust salt, pepper and a little acid one step at a timeCheck stock strength; season at the end
Too sweetNaturally sweet pumpkin, carrot or sweet potatoSmall amounts of lemon or lime juice, salt or a savoury spice; assess each separatelyChoose add-ins to suit the sweetness you want
Stringy or grainyFibrous flesh, skin, or pieces not fully softenedCheck tenderness and fibres; keep cooking if needed, then reblend safelyPeel for consistency; cut pieces evenly
Catching / scorching at the bowl baseToo little liquid, residue, wrong settings, mixture too thickStop and follow your model's instructions; don't scrape a hot running bowl or keep heating a scorched mixtureUse an appropriate liquid level and verified settings
Splashing / steam escaping when blendingOverfilled bowl, speed raised too fast, wrong lid accessoryStop and follow the manual. Never force the lid open or cover an opening with a tea towelStay under max fill; start low, increase speed gradually
